### audit-logging

- **AU-2** — Event logging is enabled for all management-plane actions
  - *Evidence:* is_multi_region_trail and include_global_service_events asserted in tests
- **AU-6** — Audit records are available for near-real-time review and analysis
  - *Evidence:* cloud_watch_logs_group_arn set; delivery role and log group asserted in tests
- **AU-9** — Audit information is protected from unauthorized modification
  - *Evidence:* enable_log_file_validation true; bucket versioning and Object Lock COMPLIANCE enforced
- **AU-11** — Audit records retained per organizational retention policy
  - *Evidence:* retention_days validated >= 365 at plan time
- **AC-6** — Least privilege applied to the audit delivery role
  - *Evidence:* log-stream-scoped resource ARN, no wildcard actions, optional permissions boundary
- **SC-8** — Transmission confidentiality enforced for audit record transfer
  - *Evidence:* explicit Deny on aws:SecureTransport=false in both bucket policies
- **SI-4** — System monitoring emits notifications on audit record creation
  - *Evidence:* SNS topic subscribed to s3:ObjectCreated with an encrypted topic and scoped policy

**Documented exceptions**

- `CKV_AWS_18` on `aws_s3_bucket.access_logs` — This bucket is the server-access-log target; self-logging creates a write loop
- `CKV_AWS_145` on `aws_s3_bucket.access_logs` — S3 server access logging does not support CMK-encrypted targets; SSE-S3 applied
- `CKV_AWS_144` on `aws_s3_bucket.audit` — Cross-region replication is a caller-level availability decision, not a tamper-evidence control
- `CKV_AWS_144` on `aws_s3_bucket.access_logs` — Access logs are not the audit record of record
- `CKV2_AWS_62` on `aws_s3_bucket.access_logs` — Notifications proportional to read volume, no detection value

### iam-baseline

`terraform/modules/iam-baseline` — `AC-2`, `AC-6`, `AC-6(5)`, `AC-4`, `IA-2`, `IA-2(1)`, `IA-5`, `IA-5(1)`, `AU-9`

- **AC-2** — Account management constrains creation of principals and credentials
  - *Evidence:* boundary denies iam:CreateUser, iam:CreateRole, iam:CreateLoginProfile
- **AC-6** — Least privilege enforced by a ceiling that attached policies cannot exceed
  - *Evidence:* permission boundary attached at role creation; boundary_arn output asserted
- **AC-6(5)** — Privilege escalation paths are closed, not merely unused
  - *Evidence:* DenyBoundaryAlteration denies policy attachment and boundary removal
- **AC-4** — Resource creation confined to approved regions
  - *Evidence:* aws:RequestedRegion condition with global services exempted
- **IA-2** — Workload authentication uses federated identity, not static credentials
  - *Evidence:* OIDC provider and AssumeRoleWithWebIdentity trust policy; no access keys issued
- **IA-2(1)** — Federated trust is bound to fully-qualified subjects
  - *Evidence:* StringEquals on the sub claim; variable validation rejects wildcards at plan time
- **IA-5** — Static credential creation is denied at the boundary
  - *Evidence:* DenyStaticCredentialCreation covers access keys and login profiles
- **IA-5(1)** — Password policy sets length, complexity, reuse and age
  - *Evidence:* aws_iam_account_password_policy with 14-character floor and 24-password history
- **AU-9** — Audit infrastructure cannot be disabled by principals inside the boundary
  - *Evidence:* DenyAuditTampering covers CloudTrail, Config, GuardDuty, Security Hub and KMS deletion

**Documented exceptions**

- `CKV_AWS_286` on `aws_iam_policy.boundary` — Escalation is closed by DenyBoundaryAlteration; a resource-scoped Allow cannot express a boundary ceiling
- `CKV_AWS_287` on `aws_iam_policy.boundary` — Credential-exposure actions are explicitly denied in DenyStaticCredentialCreation
- `CKV_AWS_288` on `aws_iam_policy.boundary` — Exfiltration actions remain bounded by each role's identity policy; the boundary grants nothing on its own

- `CKV_AWS_290` on `aws_iam_policy.boundary` — Write actions inside a boundary are capped by the attached identity policy; effective permission is the intersection
